# v0.4.11 — Public web booking flow

Released **14 November 2026**.

## Summary

The second slice of the v0.5.0 guest-experience milestone. A guest can now book
directly from the website at `/book/`, without opening the chat or starting a
voice call, and choose the standard rate or a named rate plan before confirming.

## What's new

### Direct booking at `/book/`

- A public page validates the room type and dates through
  `search_availability`, so the quote, restrictions and rate-plan totals match
  the chat and voice channels.
- The summary shows the room type, check-in/out dates, guest count and number
  of nights.
- The guest chooses the standard rate or any applicable named `RatePlan`
  (percentage or fixed adjustment, refundable or non-refundable), with the total
  for the stay shown next to each option.
- Guest details (name, email, phone, special requests) are validated in the
  page; at least an email address or phone number is required.
- `POST /book/` creates the booking through `create_booking(source=web)` and
  confirms it immediately through `confirm_booking()`. The guest is redirected
  to the normal signed booking page, where the confirmation email and calendar
  sync follow the existing after-commit path.
- `BookingError` messages (no availability, capacity, contact, restrictions) are
  rendered as a translated notice instead of a 500.

### Entry points

- The landing hero now offers **Book a room** as the primary action.
- Every room card has **Book this room**, prefilled with that room type.
- Availability results now show **Book now** alongside **Ask the concierge** and
  **Call**; the booking link carries the room type, dates and guest count.

### Polish catalogue

- New booking-flow strings are translated; the catalogue contains 555 strings
  with 0 missing translations.

## Known limits

- There is no payment or deposit step; the web form confirms the reservation
  immediately after the guest submits their details.
- Rate plans are a radio list, not a side-by-side comparison matrix.
- The page has no availability calendar widget; dates are entered directly or
  arrive prefilled from the landing-page search.
- The booking flow does not create a guest account automatically. The guest can
  request a magic link at `/my-bookings/` with the same email address.
