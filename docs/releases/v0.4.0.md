# v0.4.0 — Revenue and delivery hardening

Released **13 November 2026**.

## Summary

v0.4.0 builds on the guest self-service release by making pricing and
operations less manual and closing the remaining delivery gaps: rate-calendar
restrictions and named rate plans, a CSV export, a documented backup/restore
drill, nightly calendar reconciliation on the scheduler, and a resend action
for confirmation emails.

## What's new

### Rate plans and restrictions

- `RatePlan` model: named plans (hotel-wide or per room type) with a
  percentage or fixed adjustment applied on top of the calculated or
  daily-rate price.
- `DailyRate` gained `closed`, `closed_to_arrival` and `max_nights`
  restrictions. `quote()` raises `date_closed`, `closed_to_arrival` or
  `max_nights`; availability search omits room types that fail.
- `GET /api/v1/pricing/` and availability accept `rate_plan`; bookings store
  the chosen plan and re-price correctly on modification.
- Availability responses include a `rate_plans` array with per-plan totals.

### Nightly reconciliation and alerts

- The scheduler sidecar now runs `reconcile_calendars --apply` after hold
  cleanup.
- `/ops/` shows a calendar-drift counter (failed, stale and unsynced events).

### Booking and revenue export

- `manage.py export_bookings --from/--to [-o file] [--status]` writes CSV.

### Backup and restore

- `docs/BACKUP.md` documents SQLite backup, restore and a quarterly
  verification drill.

### Resend confirmation email

- Booking admin gained a "Resend confirmation email" action.

## Upgrade notes

1. Apply the migrations:

   ```powershell
   .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe manage.py migrate
   ```

2. Optionally seed rate plans in the admin.

3. Refresh the Polish catalogue if deploying from source.

## Known limits

- Named rate plans are data-driven but not yet exposed in the guest-facing
  booking page UI; guests select a plan via the API.
- Transactional email provider (SES/Postmark) with bounce handling remains a
  follow-up; SMTP is still the default.
- Closed-to-arrival / max-stay restrictions are edited in the admin form; the
  month grid still focuses on rates and minimum stay.
