# v0.6.8 — flexible date parsing for chat and voice

**Released:** 2026-11-14

## Summary

Improves understanding of natural-language booking dates in the offline/demo agent and makes tool date handling more tolerant, so guests can express stays in common formats without needing an LLM or extra dependencies.

## Changes

- **Demo/provider date parsing** (`apps/providers/demo.py`)
  - Parses ranges and dates without an explicit year and with ordinals (e.g. `check in 20 of November check out 22 of November`, `arrival on the 20th of November and check out on the 22nd of November`); year is inferred from today (next year if the date has passed).
  - Parses relative start with duration (e.g. `from tomorrow for 5 days`, `od jutra na 3 noce`) as check-in plus stay length.
  - Handles Polish relative forms such as `jutra`/`pojutrze` and additional month/date phrasing alongside existing ISO and numeric formats.
- **Tool date normalization** (`apps/agents/tools.py`)
  - `ToolExecutor._date` accepts common natural date forms as a safety net for tool/LLM inputs, in addition to ISO and relative keywords.
- **Agent prompts** (`apps/agents/defaults.py`)
  - EN/PL system prompts clarify conversion of natural and relative dates/durations to ISO before tool calls.
- **Tests** (`tests/test_providers.py`)
  - Added coverage for natural-language date ranges and relative start + duration parsing.
